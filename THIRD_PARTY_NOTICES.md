# Third-party notices

Branchline includes third-party software whose licenses remain in effect independently
of Branchline's proprietary application terms.

Release packages will carry the notices for the components actually included in that
version under `Branchline.app/Contents/Resources/ThirdPartyNotices`. In Finder, use
**Show Package Contents** on the app to reach them.

The current packaging includes notices for libgit2 and SwiftTerm. A release's bundled
notices are the version-specific reference; this page does not replace them or grant
additional rights to the application. No application package is published here yet.
